5. Marketing emails
We use MailerLite to send our newsletter and the emails you ask for [confirm single or double opt-in]. We expect to email about [weekly: confirm].
Every email carries an unsubscribe link and a “stop all emails” link. Either one stops our marketing at once.
We create our own lists. Each person ticks a clear box and we keep a record of when and how. We do not buy lists.
We remove subscribers who have not opened any email for 24 months.
Email open and click tracking is used only if you have agreed to it [or: is switched off].
We market other products to WIZ users only through our website and email, and only with your consent. We never use what you tell WIZ for marketing.
We do not send marketing by text, WhatsApp or phone.
Do-not-contact list. If you unsubscribe or ask us to stop emailing you, we keep your email address on a “do not contact” list so that we do not email you again by mistake. This is the only information we keep for this purpose, and we use it only to respect your choice. We keep it for as long as we send email marketing, because removing it would put you at risk of being contacted again. We do this because it is in your interest and ours to respect your choice (legitimate interests), and because the law on electronic marketing expects it. If you want your address removed from this list too, contact info@mindflowpro.com. We will explain what that means: we can no longer guarantee that you will not be contacted if your address reaches us again.

9. How we protect your information
We use measures suited to the sensitivity of what we hold: two-step sign-in and strong passwords on key accounts, encrypted devices, limited access, encryption of data in transit and at rest [confirm], approved suppliers under contract, secure handling by our developer, and a breach procedure. If a breach puts you at risk we will tell the ICO within 72 hours where required, and tell you without undue delay if the risk to you is high. No system is perfectly secure.
10. Your rights
You have the right to be informed; to see the information we hold and get a copy; to have wrong information corrected; to have information deleted, in many cases; to restrict our use of it; to receive information you gave us in a portable format; to object, including always to marketing; to withdraw consent at any time; and to ask for human review of any significant automated decision (we make none).
To use a right, email info@mindflowpro.com. WIZ users will be able to download or delete their data in the app [planned: Eddie to build before launch]. We reply within one month, free of charge. We may ask you to prove who you are. The month can be paused while we wait for that, or extended by up to two months if the request is complex, and we will tell you why. We search for your information in a reasonable and proportionate way.
If you live in the EU, EU data protection law also applies to our handling of your information and you have the same rights.
11. Complaints
Please tell us first: info@mindflowpro.com, or the complaint form on our website or in WIZ. We acknowledge a complaint about personal information within 5 working days (and in any case within 30 days) and give you the outcome without undue delay. See our Complaints Procedure. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at any time: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113. If you live in the EU you can complain to your national data protection authority.
12. Changes
We may update this notice. The current version and its date are always on our website and linked in WIZ. If a change matters to you, we will tell you, and we will ask for fresh consent where the law requires it.
