Created to help you achieve Peak Performance in anything

Mind Flow State is the ability to access effortless focus, calm confidence and accelerated performance……at will.

Athletes call it being in the zone. Psychologists call it Flow.

We call it Mind Flow because it can be trained. It is built on practical techniques and a clear methodology, it is repeatable, and it is available to everyone, drawing on 35 years of experience.

Prefer to jump straight in? You can always explore the rest later.

WHAT IS WIZ?

WIZ is a coaching intelligence. It’s not a course you have to follow, not a questionnaire you have to complete and not simply a chatbot waiting for you to ask it a question.

WIZ is your thinking partner.

You can bring WIZ a problem, a question, an ambition, a decision, or simply a feeling that something needs to change.

You don’t have to know exactly what you need before you begin.

There is no fixed agenda. No judgement. No advice you didn’t ask for.

You decide where the conversation goes.

You work together with WIZ – and WIZ learns to understand you.

Start with what’s on your mind.
WIZ will help you explore it.

HOW DOES WIZ WORK?

There is no complicated process.

01
You start

Tell WIZ what’s on your mind. You don’t need to know exactly what you need.

02
WIZ explores

WIZ asks questions, offers a different perspective where useful, and helps you look at things from new angles.

03
You understand

Together you identify patterns, strengths, obstacles, opportunities and possibilities.

04
You decide

When you’re ready, WIZ helps you turn insight into practical next steps — and a personal report you can keep. WIZ may also offer a short in-app training session on a relevant topic.

The conversation develops around you. Not the other way around.

WHAT MIGHT YOU GET FROM WIZ?

Every person’s experience will be different. But here is what WIZ is designed to offer:

1Clarity — when things feel uncertain or confused.
2Self-understanding — a better sense of your thoughts, behaviours, strengths and patterns.
3Perspective — seeing a situation differently.
4Confidence — understanding what may be holding you back and what you can do about it.
5Performance — developing how you think, learn, work, compete or perform.
6Direction — working out what you want and where you might go next.
7Action — turning insight into something practical.
